Local Data Processing (NMR Files) The ShiftBench application is designed to read and display NMR spectroscopy files (such as Bruker, Varian, and JCAMP-DX formats). All files opened through the app are processed exclusively locally on your device. No files, spectra, or scientific data are uploaded to external servers, stored in the cloud by the Developer, or shared with third parties. Your research and data remain strictly confidential and confined to your smartphone. 2. Data Collection and Use (Crashlytics) To improve the experience and stability of the application, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Specifically, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google). In the event of an app error or crash, Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects anonymous log data from your phone. This data includes technical information such as the device's IP address, device name, operating system version (Android), app configuration at the time of the crash, and the time the issue occurred. This data is used exclusively to diagnose and fix software bugs. Links to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app: Google Play Services Firebase Crashlytics 3.
